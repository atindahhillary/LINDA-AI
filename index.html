The Problem

Threats nobody is mapping in real time

Laikipia and Samburu lose people, livestock, land, and income to six compounding threats.

Banditry & armed conflict

Raids displace families with no early-warning system in place.

Livestock theft

The single biggest driver of pastoralist income loss in the region.

Human-wildlife conflict

Crop and livestock losses erode trust in conservation efforts.

Illegal grazing & resource conflict

Competition over land and water turns violent during drought.

Drought shocks

Climate shocks compound every other threat on this list.

Road insecurity & tourism decline

Insecure transport routes drain the region's tourism economy.

The Solution

One platform: mapped, analyzed, actionable

LINDAI turns scattered incident reports into a live, GIS-mapped picture of risk. Every feature ties back to measurable economic impact, not just security counts. Role-based dashboards put the right picture in front of the people who actually have to respond — from a ranger on patrol to a county emergency desk.

Modular microservices

Built to extend, not rebuild, as new regions and partners come on.

Offline-first

Designed for the connectivity these communities actually have.

Real-time analytics

Incidents resolve to the map as they're reported.

Enterprise-grade security

Role-based access built in from day one, not bolted on.

Built on Claude API, with proprietary local data and workflows layered on top.
